7-Minute Meal Prep for Busy Professionals

When your calendar is wall-to-wall, nutrition is the first habit to slip. This 7-minute meal prep system helps you assemble balanced meals-fast-without weighing food or counting every calorie. And with Nutrilogix's AI photo analysis, you can snap a plate and get instant macros, so tracking stays effortless.

The 7-minute formula: Protein + Veg + Smart Carb + Flavor. Build your plate using quick-cook or ready-to-eat staples, then snap a photo in the app to verify balance and get smart tweaks.

The 7-Minute Framework

Think in modules. Keep a few options from each bucket on hand so you can rotate meals without decision fatigue. Use hand portions as a visual guide, then let the app refine portions with photo analysis.

Protein (1-2 palms)

  • Quick-cook: Rotisserie chicken, canned tuna/salmon, pre-cooked chicken strips
  • No-cook: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, hard-boiled eggs, deli turkey
  • Plant-based: Tofu/tempeh, pre-cooked lentils, chickpeas

Vegetables (2 fists)

  • Raw: Bagged salads, baby spinach, slaw mix, cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, pre-cut peppers
  • Cooked: Microwave steam-in-bag veggies (broccoli, green beans, mixed veg)
  • Roasted: Pre-made roasted Brussels sprouts or sweet potato from the deli counter

Smart Carbs (1 fist)

  • Instant brown rice, microwaveable quinoa packets, sweet potato
  • Whole grain bread, oats, legume-based pasta
  • Beans and lentils (canned for speed)

Flavor & Fat (thumb)

  • Olive oil, avocado, nuts, seeds
  • Salt, pepper, lemon, garlic powder, hot sauce
  • Vinaigrettes, salsa, tahini

Sample 7-Minute Meals

Here are three real examples that take minutes to assemble:

Meal 1: Protein Bowl

Rotisserie chicken + microwave brown rice + bagged salad + olive oil drizzle + lemon

Macros: ~40g protein, 45g carbs, 12g fat | 450 cals

Meal 2: Tuna Plate

Canned tuna + sweet potato + steamed broccoli + avocado

Macros: ~35g protein, 48g carbs, 15g fat | 480 cals

Meal 3: Mediterranean

Greek yogurt + granola + cucumber + tomato + feta + olive oil

Macros: ~25g protein, 52g carbs, 14g fat | 460 cals

Pro Tip: Prep your protein and carbs in bulk on Sunday (roast chicken, cook rice), then mix and match throughout the week. On busy days, this cuts your prep time to just 2 minutes.
